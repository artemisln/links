Chrome extensions

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Overview

This policy covers two Chrome extensions developed by Artemis Codes: OG Preview: Social Card Debugger and PNG Image Downloader. They do not collect, store, sell or share personal data.

OG Preview: Social Card Debugger

  • When you click the toolbar icon, the extension reads the current page's address, title and meta tags so it can show a preview. This happens inside your browser.
  • The information is shown in the popup and is gone when the popup closes. It is not saved and not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The popup shows the preview image the page points to, so your browser loads that image from the page's own host, as the page itself would.

PNG Image Downloader

  • The extension runs on a tab only after you turn it on with the toolbar icon. It then shows a download button over images.
  • When you click that button, the extension fetches that one image, converts it to PNG in your browser and saves it to your Downloads folder. The image is requested from the site that hosts it, the same way your browser would load it.
  • The extension keeps a list of which tabs have it switched on, in the browser's session storage, so the button stays on after you open another page in that tab. The list is cleared when you close the browser and is never sent anywhere.
  • Optional access to all sites is requested once so the extension can read images hosted on other domains. If you decline, it works only for images from the page's own site.

What we never do

  • No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking in either extension
  • No data is sold or transferred to third parties
  • No data is used for advertising, credit or lending decisions, or anything unrelated to the extensions' single purposes
  • No code is loaded from outside the extension package

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the changes on this page with an updated revision date.
