Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 15, 2026

Overview

Μία Νύχτα στο Παλέρμο ("One Night in Palermo") is a social deduction party game developed by Artemis Codes. Your privacy is important to us.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. This app:

  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use analytics or tracking services
  • Does not contain advertisements
  • Does not use third-party SDKs that collect data
  • Does not require an account or login
  • Does not make any network requests
  • Does not access your contacts, photos, or any other device data

Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Player names you enter (for convenience when starting new games)
  • Your game settings preferences (language, enabled roles)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any data from any users, including children. The app is suitable for users of all ages, though the game's social deduction theme involves fictional mild themes.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the changes on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at artemis.leona@gmail.com.
