Frequently Asked Questions

How many players do I need?

The game supports 5 to 20 players. Additional roles (Doctor, Informant, Lovers) are recommended for 6-7+ players.

Do I need internet to play?

No. The app works completely offline. All game data is stored locally on your device.

Does everyone need a phone?

No. Only one device is needed. Players pass the phone around during the role reveal phase.

What languages are supported?

The app is fully available in Greek and English. You can switch languages in the settings.

How do I delete my data?

Simply uninstall the app. All data (player names, settings) is stored only on your device and is removed when the app is deleted.
