Topics

What I pitch, and what I am happy to tailor for your audience.

AI agents in production

Production safety for autonomous systems, and the guardrails that make them survivable: blast radius, reversibility, and policy before execution. Drawn from building the Context and Control Model at NOFire AI.

What breaks when AI writes your code

The failure modes I actually hit shipping AI-assisted code, and what I check before any of it reaches production. Review habits, test coverage that earns its keep, and where the model reliably lets you down.

The Gen Z builder path

Vibe coding to production engineering: how the next generation learns to build, why the traditional path is inverting, and what that means for engineering culture and for hiring juniors.

Formats: conference talk, lightning talk, hands-on workshop, or panel. In person or remote.
